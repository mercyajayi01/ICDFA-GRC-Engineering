# Lab 2 – NexaLearn GRC Capability Assessment

**Course:** GRC101 – Introduction to Governance, Risk, and Compliance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Scenario

NexaLearn Digital Services Limited, a Nigerian ed-tech company serving roughly 18,000
learners (around 600 in the EEA), is scaling fast: the Board wants a 40% increase in
active learners within a year and plans to roll out an AI-assisted admissions screening
tool within three months. Existing governance gaps — including shared administrator
accounts and a former contractor's account that remained active — needed to be assessed
and addressed.

## What this lab covered

- Learn, Align, Perform, Review phases of building a defensible GRC capability
- Assessing NexaLearn's current governance, risk, and compliance posture
- Building a risk register and control testing evidence
- Producing a formal GRC assessment report
- Producing a GRC workbook covering context, risk register, obligations, a compliance
  control framework, control tests, a dashboard, and an action plan
- Writing a one-page Executive Decision Brief summarizing findings and recommendations for
  the Board

## Contents of this folder

- `GRC101_Lab2_Assessment_Report.pdf` – the full NexaLearn GRC Assessment Report
- `GRC101_Lab2_Executive_Decision_Brief.pdf` – the one-page Executive Decision Brief
- `GRC101_Lab2_GRC_Workbook.xlsx` – the GRC workbook (risk register, obligations,
  control framework, control tests, dashboard, action plan)
- `Evidence/` – control test and evidence-table screenshots supporting the assessment
