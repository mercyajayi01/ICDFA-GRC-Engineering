# Lab 3 – Risk Management Fundamentals

**Course:** GRC101 – Introduction to Governance, Risk, and Compliance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Objective

Apply the fundamentals of cybersecurity risk management, from identification through
treatment planning.

## What this lab covered

- Core concepts of risk management in cybersecurity
- Risk identification, assessment, and mitigation methodologies
- Building a risk matrix and prioritizing risks
- Developing a risk treatment plan
