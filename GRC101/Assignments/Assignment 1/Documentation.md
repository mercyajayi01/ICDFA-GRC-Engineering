# Assignment 1 – GRC Frameworks and Principles

**Course:** GRC101 – Introduction to Governance, Risk, and Compliance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Objective

A theory-based assignment covering the fundamental concepts of Governance, Risk, and
Compliance (GRC), comparing GRC frameworks, and applying GRC principles to real-world
scenarios.

## What this assignment covered

- Defining Governance, Risk, and Compliance and explaining how the three components
  are interconnected
- Comparing different GRC frameworks
- Applying GRC principles to practical, real-world scenarios
- Critical evaluation of GRC implementation approaches
