# Assignment 2 – Foundations of Integrated GRC

**Course:** GRC101 – Introduction to Governance, Risk, and Compliance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Objective

Explore why Governance, Risk, and Compliance function best as an integrated capability
rather than as separate, siloed activities, and the challenges that arise when they are
kept apart.

## What this assignment covered

- How governance, risk management, and compliance are distinct but interconnected
  organizational capabilities
- The challenges caused by treating GRC as separate departments, including duplicated
  effort and conflicting ownership of responsibilities
- Applying integrated GRC thinking to a real-world organizational scenario
