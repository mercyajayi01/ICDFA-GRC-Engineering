# Lab 3 – TechGlobal Governance Architecture

**Course:** GRC102 – Information Security Governance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Scenario

TechGlobal, a company with roughly 2,500 staff across five offices, had security decisions
centred on a single IT Director with no formal committee structure and no clear reporting
to the Board. As Lead Security Governance Consultant, the task was to design a governance
structure that could scale with the company.

## What this lab covered

- Governance architecture and stakeholder mapping
- Governance responsibility and authority matrix
- Security governance committee ecosystem (three committee levels)
- RACI accountability matrix for 15 key activities
- Cyber-risk escalation workflow, from operations to the Board
- Segregation of duties review and remediation
