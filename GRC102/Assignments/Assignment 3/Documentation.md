# Assignment 3 – Cross-Functional Governance, the CISO, and Breach Case Study

**Course:** GRC102 – Information Security Governance
**Institution:** International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA)

## Scenario

Acting as Lead Security Governance Adviser for a large, fast-growing organization where
security decisions were still treated mainly as an IT responsibility.

## What this assignment covered

- **Part A** – Cross-functional governance responsibility model: eight governance roles, a
  RACI matrix, segregation of duties, a material cyber risk escalation path, and a
  cross-functional failure scenario
- **Part B** – How the CISO role has evolved, CISO reporting lines and Board access,
  regulatory scrutiny, and a public example (Uber and Joseph Sullivan)
- **Part C** – Governance case study of the 2024 Change Healthcare cyberattack: incident
  overview, governance weaknesses, and recommended improvements
- **Part D** – NDPC data analysis and GRC application, with supporting charts
